A Love for Jazz

The music that changed how I hear everything else

Jazz found me at an odd moment. I was listening to Miles Davis's Kind of Blue and something clicked — the way the musicians leave space, the way silence becomes part of the sound. It's a music built on conversation, and once you start hearing it that way, you can't unhear it.

The recordings from the 1950s and 60s feel alive in a way that's hard to explain. They were mostly recorded in a single room on one or two microphones — people playing together in real time. That electricity still comes through six decades later.
