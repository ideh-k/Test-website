How to Craft a Diamond Sword

The diamond sword is one of the strongest weapons in Minecraft. It deals 7 attack damage per hit and lasts for 1,562 uses before it breaks.

Don't skip the enchanting table!

Full disclosure, I learned this from the Minecraft Wiki and you can view the original page by clicking the link below.


Original Recipe

Materials

  • 2 Diamonds
  • 1 Stick
  • A Crafting Table

Crafting Steps

  1. Open your crafting table.
  2. Place 1 diamond in the top-center slot of the crafting grid.
  3. Place 1 diamond in the middle-center slot.
  4. Place 1 stick in the bottom-center slot.
  5. Drag the diamond sword from the result box into your inventory.
